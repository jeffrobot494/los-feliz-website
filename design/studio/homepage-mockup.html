Why the hero changed: the first version used a full-bleed cinematic photo with text laid over it. LFAR's real photo library is phone snapshots — mostly vertical, mixed lighting, no clear space for text. A split hero uses those photos at their natural shape and keeps the headline perfectly legible, so the homepage still looks good on the day a volunteer uploads a phone picture. If LFAR can commit to one professionally shot wide image, the full-bleed version is worth revisiting.

Beyond adoption

We help the animals nobody else shows up for.

Found an injured hawk? A litter under your porch? A dog with no collar wandering Hillhurst? Most of what we do never involves an adoption at all — and you don't have to be a foster or a donor to ask us for help.
