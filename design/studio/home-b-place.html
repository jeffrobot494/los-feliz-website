The neighbourhood

We don't have kennels. We have living rooms.

There's no shelter floor here and no rows of cages. Sixty-two households between Los Feliz, Silver Lake and Atwater keep our animals until they're placed — which is why we can tell you what a cat is like at 6am, not just how it behaves in a kennel.
