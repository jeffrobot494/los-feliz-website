The questions everyone actually has

What if I get attached?

You will. Every foster does, and it's the reason you're good at it. Most fosters describe the goodbye as hard and worth it — and you get first refusal if you decide you can't.

Who pays for vet care?

We do. All of it, including emergencies. You never front money for an animal in our care.

What if it isn't working out?

Call us and we'll move the animal. No guilt, no lecture. A foster who bails once and comes back is worth more to us than one who burns out.

Can I go on vacation?

Yes. Tell us your dates and we'll arrange respite care, or match you with an animal whose timeline fits around you.

I have my own pets. Is that a problem?

Usually not. We'll match you with an animal that suits your household, and walk you through a slow introduction.

Finding: 33% of ~2,000 people surveyed became more interested in fostering purely from being told what it does — most people simply don't know. A third reported anxiety and worry. And 73% trust a friend who fosters over any official source. So this block explains the impact, answers the fear in the fosterer's own words, names flexible commitments, and ends in a conversation rather than an application. (Edge Research / Maddie's Fund)
