Los Feliz Animal Rescue

Los Feliz Animal Rescue is dedicated to creating a community full of healthy and happy animals, regardless of species or domestication.

We want to accomplish this by:

  • Providing resources to the public, including information on:
    • How to coexist with wildlife
    • What to do when you find a lost or hurt animal
    • Free and low cost pet resources
  • Supporting independent rescuers by providing a location for them to market their animals, connect with fosters, and borrow equipment as needed.

Happy Tales

Success Story: Pepper

Pepper came to us as a nervous stray puppy and spent four months with a foster family learning to trust people. Her foster taught her to walk on a leash, meet new dogs and sleep through the night.

She's been home with her family for a year now, and she has two kids to play with every afternoon. Every adoption starts with a neighbor who opened their home.
