Good questions

Do I need experience with animals?

No. Plenty of our volunteers had never fostered or trained a dog. We'll show you what to do, and your first shift is with someone who's done it before.

How much time do I have to give?

As much or as little as you like. Some volunteers do one vet ride a month; others work every adoption event. You can pause any time.

I'm allergic, or my building doesn't allow pets. Can I still help?

Yes. Sharing posts, supply runs, photography at events and skills work like design or grant writing don't need you to have an animal at home.

Can my kids help?

[Confirm] Teens can volunteer at events with a parent or guardian. Younger kids are welcome to hold a fundraiser or a supply drive.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. We're a registered 501(c)(3), EIN 87-4677140. Donorbox emails your receipt automatically.
