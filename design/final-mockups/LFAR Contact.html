Before you ask

Can I drop off an animal?

We don't have a building. Every animal lives with a foster family, so we can only take in an animal when a foster home is open. Send us a message first and we'll tell you what we can do.

Can you pick up a stray?

Usually not. We don't have staff or vehicles on call. The lost and found page explains how to check for a microchip and who to contact.

Do you have a phone number?

We don't have a public phone line. The form or email reaches the whole team, so it's the fastest way to get an answer.

Can I come meet an animal?

Yes. After you apply, we set up a meet-and-greet with the animal's foster family.

Where can I send a tax receipt question?

Donation receipts come from Donorbox by email. If yours didn't arrive, pick "Something else" in the form and we'll sort it out.

Send Us a Message

Questions, offers of help, press, or just a photo of an alum doing well. It all comes here.

  • Social

    Direct messages are checked less often than email.

  • Where we are

    Los Feliz, Silver Lake and Atwater Village, Los Angeles. Our animals live in foster homes, so there's no public address. Meet-and-greets are set up after you apply.

  • When we reply

    Usually within 2–3 days. Adoption and foster questions go to the top of the pile.

Who reads your message?

Our founder and a small group of volunteers. We keep your details in our volunteer records so we can follow up, and never share them.
