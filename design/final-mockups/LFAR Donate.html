What Does Your Donation Buy?

Meet Pepper. A neighbor found her wandering near Griffith Park and took her home that night as our newest foster.

Her foster home didn't cost a thing. Everything else she needs takes donations. Follow her rescue and see what yours buys.

Follow Pepper's rescue ↓
Day 1 · Found near Griffith Park $0Foster home: free

  1. Day 2

    A full bowl

    Puppy food, a few treats and a bed that's all hers. She eats like she's been waiting all week for it.

    $10buys her first week of food and bedding

  2. Week 1

    Her first shots

    Her first vet visit, and her first round of puppy vaccines. She handles it like a champ, especially the treat afterward.

    $25buys her vaccines

  3. Week 3

    A chip for life

    A full checkup and a microchip, so if she ever slips out the gate, she can always find her way home.

    $50buys a vet exam and microchip

  4. Week 6

    Surgery day

    Her spay surgery, so she's ready for adoption. She's back to her zoomies by the weekend.

    $100buys her spay surgery

  5. Week 8

    Everything else

    Dewormer, flea care and a follow-up visit to make sure she's growing the way she should.

    $65covers the rest of her care

  6. Adoption day

    Pepper goes home

    Two kids, a backyard and a family that fell for her on the first visit. Her whole rescue came to $250, paid in full by neighbors who gave what they could.

    $250one whole rescue
