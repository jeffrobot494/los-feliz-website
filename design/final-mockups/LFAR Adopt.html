Someone Who Always Has Time for You

Long workdays, packed weekends and a phone that never stops buzzing. It's harder than ever to make time for the connections that recharge us.

Everyone does better with someone to come home to.
See what helps

Connection Recharges Us

Feeling close to someone is part of what keeps us going, like good sleep or sunshine.

  • More energy

    Time with someone you love is how we refuel after a long week.

  • Less stress

    A friendly face at the end of the day makes the hard parts easier to shake off.

  • Better days

    People with close bonds tend to be happier, healthier and more focused.

The catch is that real connection takes time, and time is the one thing busy people are short on.

Some of the best company fits right into your life.

The Answer Has Four Legs

A rescue animal doesn't need a calendar invite. They're there when you wake up and waiting when you get home, and they make the rest of your day better too.

  • Someone happy to see you, every dayPets are built-in company, and time with them eases stress and anxiety.
  • A healthier heartWalking and playing with a pet can lower blood pressure and cholesterol.
  • A reason to get outside every dayMore walks, more fresh air, more routine.
  • New friends on the blockA dog on a leash starts a lot of conversations.

Health benefits as listed by the CDC, Healthy Pets, Healthy People.
